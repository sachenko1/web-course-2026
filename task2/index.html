ABOUT PORSCHE

In 2022, there were nearly 310,000 Porsche vehicles delivered to customers worldwide, with about 30% going to China, 30% to Europe, 26% to North America, and 14% to the rest of the world.

Porsche engineers and manufactures premium and luxury sports cars and sports crossovers. The Porsche brand is synonymous with motorsport, German engineering, and fast, fun to drive sports cars.

The firm also has a captive finance company that provides funding for dealers and customers, ensuring seamless acquisition of these historic driving machines.
